# AI-Assisted Quantum Gravity Research

**Status:** Research program established; source audit and mathematical reproduction are pending. No new physical discovery is claimed.

## Mission
Build an open, reproducible research program to examine candidate approaches to quantum gravity, clarify the mathematical relationship between general relativity and quantum theory, and evaluate AI-assisted discovery methods without assuming any candidate is correct.

## Start here
1. Read [the research handoff guide](CONTRIBUTING/RESEARCH_HANDOFF_GUIDE.md).
2. Read the [parallel task board](TASK_BOARD.md) and claim one bounded task.
3. Inspect primary sources and record exact versions, publication status, authorship and evidence.
4. Reproduce established calculations before testing novel AI-generated candidates.
5. Submit focused pull requests with methods, tests, uncertainty, limitations and falsifiers.

## Research tracks
- **QGR-A — Literature and claim audit:** verified bibliography and claim/evidence matrix.
- **QGR-B — Mathematical foundations:** differential geometry, general relativity, quantum theory and QFT.
- **QGR-C — Framework comparison:** effective field theory, asymptotic safety, loop quantum gravity, string/holographic, causal and discrete approaches.
- **QGR-D — Black holes and information:** thermodynamics, entropy and semiclassical limits.
- **QGR-E — AI-assisted equation discovery:** benchmarks against known results, counterexample search and reproducibility.
- **QGR-F — Observational tests:** discriminating predictions, uncertainty and feasibility.
- **QGR-G — Simulation:** versioned code, tests, environments and data provenance.

## Evidence discipline
Distinguish concept, mathematical theory, model-only result, simulation, experiment and independent reproduction. A preprint is not automatically peer reviewed; a simulation is not an experiment; an AI-generated equation is not a physical law. Every hypothesis must state assumptions, variables and units, domain, known limits, symmetry/conservation checks, observable, uncertainty and a falsifier.

## Initial priority
Start with QGR-001 (verify primary sources and claims) and QGR-002 (build the sourced mathematical baseline). Then select a bounded published calculation for independent reproduction. Do not assume the reported OpenAI/AI-assisted quantum-gravity claims in media or chat summaries are true: verify each at the primary-source level.
